## Setar

<div class="split">
<div markdown="1">
I spend most of my spare time practicing the [Setar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Setar), a traditional Persian instrument, which I have been playing since 2010. Currently, I am completing the Persian musical repertoire known as the *Radif* under the guidance of Maestro [Reza Mazandarani](https://www.instagram.com/reza_mazandarani/).

Persian music heavily relies on poetry and is inherently an aural tradition. Unlike Western music, it has only recently been documented: for centuries it was passed down orally from one generation to the next, not for lack of notation (notation existed in ancient Iran) but because of the complexity of its vocal and tonal nuances. Even today, notation is used more as a formality. If you are curious, the preface of *The Radif of Mirza Abdollah* for Tar and Setar, by Jean During, is a good introduction in both French and English.
